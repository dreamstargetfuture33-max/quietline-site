Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Who runs this

Quietline is a personal automation project operated by a single individual developer. It publishes pre-rendered video content to one TikTok account and one Instagram account, both owned and controlled by the developer. It is not offered as a service to other users.

What this app does

Quietline generates short vertical videos from a fixed narration text, licensed background footage, and licensed music, then publishes them to the developer's own TikTok and Instagram accounts on a scheduled basis using each platform's official content publishing API.

Data we access

Through TikTok Login Kit and Meta's Instagram Graph API, this app requests only the minimum OAuth permissions required to publish video content on the developer's own connected account: basic profile identification and video publishing scope. The app does not request or access follower lists, direct messages, analytics on other users, or any data belonging to accounts other than the one the developer explicitly connects.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect data from people who view the published videos.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cookies on this website.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not process data on behalf of any user other than the developer.

How access tokens are stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and Meta are stored in a private, self-hosted credential store operated by the developer and are not transmitted to any third party. Tokens are used exclusively to publish video content to the connected accounts.

Content sources

Background footage and music used in generated videos are sourced from royalty-free libraries (including Pexels, Pixabay, and Uppbeat) under licenses that permit commercial use. Quote text is original or independently authored.

Data retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app from the TikTok or Instagram account settings. Doing so immediately invalidates any stored tokens for that account. To request deletion of any residual operational data, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data relating to anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
